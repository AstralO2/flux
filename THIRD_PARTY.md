# Third-party content

Pinned Helix and DarkRP sources retain their upstream licenses and tracked patch provenance in `flux-development/vendor` at the source checkout root. Their original MIT notices are included there and in each bundled dependency directory.

The four active JPEGs under `content/materials/flux/loading/` come from SCP: Containment Breach, credited to Joonas Rikkonen (Regalis), the game contributors and the original SCP Foundation authors. Their source commit, hashes, CC BY-SA 3.0 terms and preserved full upstream credits are linked in [LOADING_IMAGES.md](docs/LOADING_IMAGES.md). These terms apply to the image assets and their adaptations; they do not relicense the separate framework Lua code.
